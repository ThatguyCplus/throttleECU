# Icarus hardware: schematic rev 0.7 + PCB (rev 0.7 routed, 100 × 100 mm)

The KiCad 10 hierarchical schematic is **generated from code**. Edit `gen/design.py`, then rebuild:

```
cd gen
python make_sch.py
```

Then from `hardware/`:

```
kicad-cli sch erc --severity-all -o erc.rpt icarus.kicad_sch
kicad-cli sch export pdf -o doc/icarus_schematic.pdf icarus.kicad_sch
```

Don't hand-edit the `.kicad_sch` files; the next build overwrites them.

## Rev 0.7 changes: diverse monitor MCU (TMS570)

CH-B moves from a second F280049C to a **TMS570LS0432-Q1** (orderable TMS5700432BPZQQ1). It's an Arm Cortex-R4F with dual cores in lockstep, ECC, CPU/RAM self-test and the ESM error pin, in the same 14 × 14 mm LQFP-100. Control (C28x) and monitor (Arm) now share no silicon, compiler or toolchain: `../docs/06_SAFETY_ELEMENT.md` F16.

Every pin comes from the datasheet (SPNS186C Tables 4-1 to 4-16); the generator checks all 100 are assigned exactly once.

| Function | Rev 0.6 (F280049C) | Rev 0.7 (TMS570) |
|---|---|---|
| Link to CH-A | FSI | **UART**: F280049C SCI-B on GPIO12/13 (the same two pins) ⇄ TMS570 SCI (LIN module) on pins 94/95, 470 Ω series each way, CRC + counter in firmware |
| Error pin into the enable gate (SM-03) | ERRORSTS (GPIO24) | **ESM nERROR** (pin 82): low = error, pulled down in reset |
| Expected-Δ reference for the guard (SM-20) | DAC-A | **N2HET[6] PWM** (~100 kHz) into the same 10 k / 1 µF RC (< 1 mV ripple); still read back on REF3M_B. The part has no DAC |
| Clock | INTOSC2 | **16 MHz quartz**, load caps to KELVIN_GND only (Note B); the crystal part is still TBD |
| Core supply | internal VREG | **TLV73312PQDRVRQ1** 1.2 V from +5V0_B (0.55 W worst case), window-supervised by **TPS3702CX12QDDCRQ1** wired into nPORRST with the TPS3850 |
| ADC | 16 of 20+ channels | All **16** MibADC inputs used; VCCAD = ADREFHI = VDDA_B (3.3 V, ferrite-filtered) |
| Debug | ARM 10-pin + UART | **TI cTI-20** JTAG (nTRST pulled down inside: TAP held in reset without a debugger). The pinout still needs checking against the XDS110 guide. No UART: the one SCI is the link |

- **Pinout traps caught:** TLV733P-Q1 has a different pin order in WSON-6 than in SOT-23. KiCad's library symbol is the SOT-23 one, so the WSON-6 symbol is custom.
- **Safety:** SM-25 (lockstep + ESM) added. FMEDA, still on placeholder rates: **SPFM 99.988 %, LFM 98.80 %, PMHF 0.086 FIT** (was 0.096). Parity: ERC 0, netlist 292 / 292.
- **Cost of the change:** CH-B firmware is now a separate HALCoGen / TI Arm codebase. That's the point of diversity, but it doubles the firmware effort.

- **Firmware notes.** The coil LEDs add ~0.9 mA to the TPS1H100 current-sense reading. The CAN LED needs pulse stretching to be visible.

## Rev 0.4 changes

- **From the throttle ECU:** TCAN3404-Q1 CAN and TPS3702 window OV/UV supervisors (both already in), SMBJ20A TVS and SQJ147 reverse FET (already in), plus a new **1812 PTC** behind the blade fuse (throttle F1).
- **SM-23, speed-dependent authority.** A CH-C analog speed stage (`guard` sheet) and a TS5A3159-Q1 that halves the overlay command above v_switch (`overlay` sheet). It fails to HALF or zero.
- **1S LiPo backup** (`backup` sheet): JST-PH → PTC → MCP73833 charger (from `+5V0_A_SRC`) → TPS61092 5 V boost → LM66100 ideal diodes into both 5 V rails. Relay coils are not backed up, on purpose.
- **SM-24:** a TPS3702 on the boost output disables both ideal diodes on overvoltage.
- **Caught and fixed during the build:** TS5A3159 COM/NC swap (full authority at high speed), TPS61091 → TPS61092 (3.3 V vs 5 V part), TPS61092 LBO pin unconnected, SN74LVC1G123 footprint (SOT-23-8 → SSOP-8 for the DCT package).

## Rev 0.3 changes (logic review + R3–R5, `../docs/06_SAFETY_ELEMENT.md`)

- **R8, authority limit moved into the summers.** OVL3 = RAW3 + k(DCMD − VMID), OVL6 = RAW6 − k(DCMD − VMID), k = 1.1 k / 10 k = 0.110, four 0.1 % values mirrored. A railed op-amp upstream of a summer is now bounded to 0.37 V (was ~3.3 V). One op-amp quad and the inverter stage removed.
- **R7, 2oo2 guard-latch clear.** SN74LVC1G08-Q1: CH-A GPIO57 (pin 66, via 1 k) AND CH-B GUARD_RST → clear FET.
- **R3, low-side guard test.** Ladder bottom through a normally-on BSS138 (gate pull-up); CH-B GPIO4 (pin 75) pulls it low to lift every threshold.
- **R4,** 100 kΩ VT_SNS→VT_EPS bypass on both lines.
- **R5,** TPS3702 SENSE through 10 k with a test FET to 2.5 V; CH-A GPIO4 (pin 75) / CH-B GPIO8 (pin 74).
- **Datasheet checks:** LDO → TPS7B8233-Q1 (300 mA; IDDIO max 113 mA). VDD (1.2 V) decoupling raised to 2 × 10 µF + 4 × 100 nF (~20 µF, per SPRS945). TPS3702CX50 with SET = VDD (4.80 / 5.20 V). TPS1H100 R_CL = 24.9 k → 99 mA limit, CS load 10 k with a 3V3 clamp. X1: single-ended clock on X1 with X2 open (CH-A); X1 to VSS when unused (CH-B). VDDIO_SW to VDDIO, VSS_SW to VSS. All per the datasheet pin table.

## Rev 0.2 changes (from the safety analysis)

- **R1, J1 re-pinned.** The torque lines now sit in a GND-guarded block (pins 6–11). The old CAN_L-next-to-VT3_SNS single-point fault is gone.
- **R2, guard referenced to CH-B's expected Δ.** New group on the `guard` sheet: CH-B DACA → RC → k attenuator → REF3/REF6, with REF3 read back on ADC pin 6. VMID_B and the window ladder (28k / 3.01k / 4.02k / 3.01k / 28k) now run from +3V3_B.

## Decisions made while drawing

- **Watchdog changed to TPS3850-Q1.** The TPS3851-Q1 listed in the concept is timeout-only, so it can't catch a watchdog serviced too early. With the TPS3850 preset (CWD 10 kΩ to VDD, SET0 = SET1 = 1), the window is 1.85 ms closed and 11 ms timeout. Its SENSE pin also monitors 3.3 V for over- and undervoltage.
- **MCU pin mux** (SPRS945 Table 5-6), the same on both channels:

  | Function | Pins |
  |---|---|
  | CAN-A | GPIO30/31 |
  | FSI TX (to the other channel) | GPIO6/7 |
  | FSI RX | GPIO12/13 |
  | SCI-A | GPIO28/29 |
  | ERRORSTS | GPIO24, into the enable AND gate |
  | eCAP inputs (VSP / NEP) | GPIO16/17 |
  | DAC-A (CH-A only) | pin 23 |

- **Reverse-battery P-FET** follows the TI SLVA139 orientation: drain to battery, source to load. The throttle board's netlist has SQJ147 **source** on the fused battery side. Check that on the throttle design; I haven't changed it.

## Open items before layout

1. **TBD-DATA values** (from the logger): Δmax / k, slew τ, guard thresholds, hold-cap value, EPS input impedance (R4).
2. **Relay.** G6K-2F-Y is a stand-in. Pick an automotive-qualified DPDT with gold contacts, and consider a different vendor for K1 and K2.
3. **Vehicle connector.** Choose the part and keep the rev 0.2 pin order (it is safety-relevant). Then build the Y-harness with Honda connector-B halves.
4. **TPS1H100 current-sense ratio K.** The datasheet text extraction lost the value; confirm it and the 10 k CS load scale (V_CS must stay < 3.3 V at the 99 mA limit; the clamp diode covers overshoot).
5. ~~TPS3702 is not automotive-qualified.~~ **Resolved (rev 0.6):** TPS3702CX50QDDCRQ1 (AEC-Q100), same package and pinout. The earlier "no -Q1 exists" note was wrong.
6. **Charge-pump gate.** By analysis the pump is a peak detector: gate ≈ 3.3 − 0.3 ≈ 3.0 V, fine for a BSS138 at 12 mA. Verify on the bench over temperature.
7. **Test points**, then the PCB: four layers, using the throttle `LAYOUT.md` rules.

## 100 × 100 mm path (rev 0.6): config A

Single-sided, 100 × 100 mm would need 45 % courtyard density before any routing room. The path:

1. **Double-sided placement.** Whole clusters go underneath, each mirrored as a unit so every cap stays at its pin.
2. **One through-hole edge strip:** J1, the blade-fuse holder and the hold-up electrolytic, kept clear on both sides. Everything else is SMD:
   - Debug headers: 1.27 mm SMD 2×5, the native pitch of the ARM 10-pin pinout.
   - UART: 1.27 mm SMD.
   - LiPo connector: JST-PH SMD side-entry.
3. **Test pads and LEDs next to their signals** (`make_pcb.place_floaters`). Each LED (with its resistor, and its blocking diode across a relay coil) and each test pad goes to the nearest free top-side spot by the pin that drives its signal.
   - If that chip is underneath, the part sits on top directly above it, so LEDs stay visible and pads probeable.
   - GND test pads sit among the probe points of their family (incoming / outgoing / measured).
   - Median 7.4 mm from the driving pin, max 23 mm.
   - Dropping the test-pad panel freed about 24 mm of width.
4. **Through-hole thermal vias** (TPS1H100 coil drivers, TPS61092 boost): their clusters share a side, which packs **last**. Only their via patches have to avoid the other side's pads; everything else packs freely.
5. **tscircuit's advice, applied:**
   - Placement is checked before routing (DRC, decoupling report).
   - Routing difficulty is estimated before routing (RUDY).
   - Gaps are tightened toward their 0.25 mm default.
   - `gen/search_floorplan.py` searches every side split and column cut against the real cluster blocks; 173 floorplans fit at 100 × 100.

**Chosen: config A.** It's the best fit of the search, with 23.8 mm spare width per side.

| Side | Columns | Density | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top | entry, pass-through, inputs · supplies, overlay · guard | 27 % | The switchers and the precision analog both sit next to the In1 GND plane |
| Bottom | coil drive, backup · CH-A, CH-B · monitors | 23 % | |

- **Routing difficulty:** RUDY p95 0.48, peak 0.91. The routed 152 × 115 board scored 0.32 / 0.67.
- **Placement DRC:** clean (only the pre-route fan-out vias).
- **Decoupling:** see the routing result below.
- **Other configs:** B (MCUs on top), C (power on top) and D (channel split) stay in `make_pcb.CONFIGS` and `ICARUS_CONFIG` for comparison.
- **Below 100 mm:** the edge strip is the limit. The 1×20 placeholder connector alone is 52 mm long, so the real vehicle connector decides it.

**Routing:** done; see "Routing result" below. **Trade-offs still open:**
- **Assembly cost:** double-sided assembly costs more at JLC.
- **Bottom-side reference plane:** parts underneath reference In1 through In2 rather than an adjacent plane. That's fine for the MCUs and logic, which is why the switchers and precision analog are on top in A.

**BOM:** `../docs/08_BOM_REVIEW.md` covers reuse from the throttle ECU and the automotive grade of every part. Rev 0.6 swapped in the verified automotive parts: TPS3702-Q1, AEC-Q200 PTCs and an NCU18 NTC.

### Layer stack: three candidates routed (rev 0.7, config A)

`ICARUS_STACK` picks the stack. In every stack the routing directions alternate from F.Cu: vertical, horizontal, vertical.

| Stack | Layers | RUDY p95 / peak | Freerouting result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SGSS** (chosen) | F sig · In1 GND · In2 sig · B sig | 0.44 / 0.79 | 122 open, 118 of them GND pads (closed by `fanout`) |
| SGSSPS | 6 layers: 4 signal, GND plane, power plane split into rail islands | 0.32 / 0.58 | 190 open (65 rails from fragmented islands) |
| SGPS | F sig · In1 GND · In2 power islands · B sig | 0.64 / 1.16 | Plateaued at 222 open after 21 passes / 3 h; stopped |

- **SGPS loses** because a power layer takes a signal layer away. The rails route as width-ruled traces on SGSS, and the third signal layer is worth more than a plane split into many small islands.
- **Six layers are not needed electrically.** SGSSPS only lowers congestion, and its split power plane routed worse than plain traces.
- The per-stack DSN, SES and log files are in `gen/route/A_<stack>.*`.

### U15 turned 180° (config A `anchor_rot`)

The first SGSS route ended with 4 connections open, all at U15 (TMS570, CH-B):
- **WLP_B, DISNC_B:** GIOA pins 9 and 10 sat on the far side of the chip from their input conditioning, walled in by the crystal and the decoupling.
- **VCC1V2_B:** the core-supply ring broke inside the pin field.
- **RAW6_A:** a neighbouring overlay net.

Neither the grid router (`bridge`) nor rip-up and reroute (`gen/rrr.py`) could open a pin walled in on every side.

Turning U15 180° fixes the cause:

| Measure | U15 at 0° | U15 at 180° |
|---|---|---|
| U15 ratsnest | 1931 mm | 1559 mm (−19 %) |
| RUDY p95 / peak | 0.44 / 0.79 | 0.41 / 0.74 |
| Cells over 0.5 | not recorded (3.0–3.1 % at spread 0.8 / 1.0) | 1.4 % |

Spreading the clusters further (`ICARUS_SPREAD` 0.8 / 1.0) didn't help: congestion stayed the same, and the floaters moved further from their pins.

### Overlay over CH-A (config A top columns)

With U15 turned, the second route still left 3 nets open round U15: SUM3P_A, RAW3_A and LINK_B2A_TX. The overlay (CH-A analog) sat on top directly over U15. Its In2 traffic walled in U15's pin field. Config A now puts the overlay over CH-A (U10) and the supplies, which have few signals, over U15. The next route left no U15 net open.

### Coil-current ADC filters at the ADC pins (`design.place_with`)

CSK2_A (K2 coil current into U10 pin 21) stayed open after two routes and every `rrr` option. Its 1 kΩ / 10 nF / clamp diode sat in the coil-drive cluster, 31 mm from the pin. They were boxed in on all three layers.

For both coils, these three parts are now placed in U10's cluster. The schematic keeps them on the relays sheet, and it regenerates byte-identical.
- **Electrically the better spot.** The 10 nF is the ADC sample-and-hold's charge reservoir and the clamp protects the pin, so both belong at the pin.
- **The long run becomes CSR.** That's the current-sense node loaded by 10 kΩ at the TPS1H100, and it's filtered at the far end.
- **Where they landed:** the 10 nF is 2.5 mm from pin 21 and all of CSK1's parts are within 4 mm of pin 29. D25 is 13 mm away, on the same short net.
